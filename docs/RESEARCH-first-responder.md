# First-responder deals and trips: niche research

Checked November 13, 2026, from public web pages. This is a first pass, not a market study. Figures are approximate and should be confirmed at the linked source before they are used in a pitch or a plan. No app was tested hands-on.

## Short version

- The audience is real and reachable: roughly two million or more people in fire, police, and EMS.
- Discounts for them are everywhere, and so are lists of those discounts. This is not an empty market.
- What the existing options do not appear to do is compare the responder price against today's public sale price. That is the opening.
- The app does not need to verify anyone. Merchants already do that at checkout through ID.me, SheerID, or GovX ID.

## Who is already here

| Player | What it is | Note |
|---|---|---|
| GovX | Members-only store for military, police, fire, and EMS | FireRescue1 describes up to 65% off across 400+ brands, plus tickets. Also sells verification to merchants (GovX ID). |
| ID.me Shop | Discount directory tied to ID.me verification | Covers career and volunteer firefighters, EMTs, paramedics, police, and 911 dispatchers. |
| SheerID | Verification used by large retailers | Not a consumer destination. |
| FireRescue1 and Police1 | Trade news sites with discount roundups | Editorial lists, updated from time to time. |
| Heroes Vacation Club | Travel club for responders | An EMS World article describes a $99 per year premium tier. Date of that article not confirmed. |
| Blog roundups | "80+ first responder discounts" style posts | Go stale quickly. |

## What the discounts look like

- Usually a flat percentage (10% to 20% is common) after ID verification.
- Travel examples found: a 10% hotel booking discount for verified responders, cruise fares that cover the responder's whole cabin, and resort and casino rates through ID.me.
- Common catches: cannot be combined with other offers, limited to one cabin or one item, blackout dates, and policies that vary by location.

## The gap

A responder discount is often worse than a public sale. A 10% responder code loses to a 30% public markdown, and the two usually cannot be stacked. None of the directories or lists reviewed showed that comparison.

What this app can do that they do not appear to:

1. **Best real price.** Show the responder price next to the best public price for the same item or trip, and say which wins.
2. **Stacking rules on the card.** "Cannot be combined," "one cabin per responder," "weekdays only."
3. **Who qualifies.** Career, volunteer, retired, dispatcher, family. Policies differ and are rarely stated in one place.
4. **How to verify.** Which service the merchant uses, and whether it is at checkout or in person.
5. **Trips.** Ski, beach, cruise, and stays with all-in cost. Most responder lists are retail-heavy.
6. **Freshness.** Posted date and age on every card, with automatic expiry. Blog lists do not expire.

## Audience size (approximate)

- Firefighters: about 1.0 million, of whom roughly 635,000 are volunteers (NVFC fact sheet, citing NFPA's 2020 fire department profile).
- Police and sheriff's officers: roughly 650,000 to 830,000 depending on the source and definition.
- EMS: published counts range from a few hundred thousand to over 800,000. Many EMS workers are also firefighters, so the totals overlap.

Treat "about two million" as a rough floor, not a measured number.

## Reaching them

- Stations, union locals, and department social groups.
- Trade sites and newsletters that responders already read.
- National First Responders Day (October 28) and the holiday season, when merchants run responder promotions.

## Risks

- **Crowded with lists.** The comparison feature has to be clearly better, or this is one more list.
- **Data.** Responder prices are rarely in affiliate feeds. Early on they will be entered by hand or submitted by users, which takes ongoing effort.
- **Trademarks.** Do not use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID logos or imply a partnership without one.
- **Accuracy.** Local discounts vary by location. Label them "varies by location" unless confirmed.
- **Affiliate conflict.** Some responder discounts cannot be used through an affiliate link. Show the better deal even when it earns nothing.

## Open questions

1. Do GovX or ID.me run affiliate or partner programs that would allow listing their offers?
2. How many responder discounts can be combined with a public sale? Sampling 30 merchants would answer this.
3. Would responders use a comparison tool? Asking 20 to 30 people at local stations would answer this cheaply before more is built.
